# Second Take · a morning edition for each financial-news audience

**The problem.** An FX trader and an AI investor open the same news feed with different questions. Both lose time hearing yesterday’s context again. Give each listener a short audio edition they can check, and a returning listener just the part they missed.

**Who pays?** A financial publisher with licensed reporting, editorial staff and an existing subscriber relationship. It can offer a useful morning habit without commissioning a separate podcast for every audience. A possible model is a publisher platform fee plus usage for new editions; price against editorial effort and repeat listening in a pilot. No customers or partnerships are claimed.

**From desk to listener.** Ingest authorized articles once → normalize and group close reports → rank events for an audience and cutoff → draft from selected source sentences → check numbers locally and verify each claim independently → link each sentence to its original article. Record only a sufficient verified script; if the MP3 misses its time window, keep the transcript and withhold audio. An insufficient edition stays explicitly transcript-only, even if native headlines are available.

**Show, don’t promise.** Stage the two gate-checked FX and Dutch AI editions at the same archive cutoff. Play both excerpts; show different event groups, not just translations. Open a source under the spoken story. In the clearly labelled fictional Memory rehearsal, mark the first edition heard, then play just the later development. No universal real-news Memory is claimed. The archive is a listener prototype with a private audit trail, not a CMS or an audio distribution network.

**Economics.** Local intake is shared: neither 1,740 articles nor a 17,400-article scenario calls a model per article. The budget follows shortlisted evidence, verified output, optional repair/recheck and new voice characters per audience edition. Published MP3 plays do not repeat generation. The [stage-by-stage cost sheet](../backend/MEASUREMENTS.md) records measured usage for the two actual demo recordings: **about $0.2148 estimated provider usage** at published standard rates, excluding failed attempts, rights and local infrastructure. At ten times the articles, fixed shortlisted output need not multiply API usage by ten; twenty equivalent cold editions would cost about $2.15 in model/voice usage.

**Publisher deployment (proposed).** Put an editor before publication, prioritizing uncertainty, repairs and conflicts. Let the editor inspect originals, withhold, correct and re-listen before distributing approved editions through the publisher’s app. Pilot with two audience desks, one curated timeline, licensed access and consent for the chosen providers. Track source corrections, repeat listens and cost per published edition. Negotiate rights for derived audio, provider processing, distribution and retention: the hackathon Newstex licence does not grant ongoing commercial rights.
