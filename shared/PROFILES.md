# Listener profiles for the demo

The Sourcera cofounder told our team on 20 November 2026 that participants need to invent the listener profiles themselves. The two presets in `listener-profiles.json` are therefore **our product choices**, not organizer-supplied personas. They were selected against the available article headlines: one focused English FX briefing and one Dutch AI/cloud briefing. They should produce different stories as well as a different language.

Person B can use these exact names and `listener` objects for the two preset buttons. Keep the custom profile form for the jury's live wildcard; do not prefill the wildcard with a preset. Show `topicMatchStatus` when a custom topic has no clear match. The preset wording is a hypothesis about user needs, not customer research.

The cofounder's reply addressed profiles only. The existence or location of the separately mentioned public releases remains unknown.
